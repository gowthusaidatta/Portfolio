Wireframe — Portfolio (black & white sketch)

Top → Bottom order:

- Nav (sticky) [Home | About | Skills | Projects | Experience | Certifications | Profiles | Contact]
- Hero (two-column on desktop: text left, portrait right)
- About (two-column: image left, bio text right)
- Skills (3-column grid desktop; cards with icon + label + skill bar)  *animated reveal*
- Projects (3-card grid: title, 2–3 line description, tech list, links)  *stagger reveal*
- Experience (single column card with date and bullets)  *animated reveal*
- Certifications (2-column cards with logo + title + link)
- Profiles (row/grid of small profile cards with logos)  *hover effect*
- Contact (grid of contact cards: email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub)
- Footer (centered small links + copyright)

Layout rough boxes (desktop):

- Nav: horizontal centered container; logo left, links right; on mobile -> hamburger -> vertical list

- Hero:
  - Left column: eyebrow, large H1, 2-line bio, CTA buttons
  - Right column: circular portrait inside decorative card (parallax background element behind)
  - Animated elements: hero text reveal (1), portrait parallax (star), CTA button hover

- About:
  - Left: photo card
  - Right: 3-paragraph bio with bold phrases
  - Animated: section reveal (2)

- Skills:
  - Grid of cards (icon, title, short text, skill-bar)
  - Animated: each card fades/slide in staggered (3+ reveals)

- Projects:
  - 3-column cards desktop -> 1-column mobile
  - Each card: title, meta (tech), short description, links
  - Animated: stagger reveal, link hover states

- Experience / Certifications / Profiles / Contact: simple cards in grid, each with reveal on-scroll

Notes on animation markers: mark reveals with an asterisk (*) where on-scroll reveals occur; parallax background gets a star (★).
